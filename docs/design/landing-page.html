Control

AI you can check, limit, and audit.

Quality is measured, not claimed. Every feature is tested against a golden dataset, including the failures.

  • Always a source. Answers carry citations, or say nothing was found.
  • AI cannot change your data. Data questions are read-only. Dangerous queries are blocked and logged.
  • Risky actions need approval. Sending email, calling outside services, or writing data waits in the Approval Center.
  • Everything is recorded. Who asked what, when, and the result. Ready for internal audit.
  • Documents are data, not commands. Hidden instructions inside a file cannot take over the assistant.

For your IT team

Installed with one command.

  • Docker Compose on-premise, on a VPS, or in a private cloud.
  • Minimum: 4 vCPU, 16 GB RAM, 50 GB disk. Recommended: 8 vCPU, 32 GB RAM, 16–24 GB GPU.
  • Local models through Ollama or vLLM. Optional external providers through a single gateway.
  • FastAPI, Next.js, PostgreSQL + pgvector, Redis, LangGraph, Langfuse. All open source.
  • Backup, restore, and upgrades with automatic migrations.

FAQ

Questions clients ask first.

Is this a subscription service (SaaS)?

No. NAWA.AI is installed and run on your own servers.

Is our data sent to an AI provider?

Not by default. Local models are the starting setup. External providers work only if an admin enables them, and never in offline mode.

What if the answer is not in our documents?

NAWA.AI says it could not find it. It does not make something up.

Can the AI change or delete our data?

No. Data access is read-only. Other risky actions wait for a person to approve them.

How accurate is it?

We measure it against a standard question set and publish the results, failures included. Accuracy on your own data is established during the pilot.

Do we need a GPU?

A GPU is recommended for a comfortable team experience. Small deployments can run on a server without one, using a small model or an optional external provider.

Can it work without internet?

Yes, with models downloaded beforehand.

What does it connect to?

Today: PostgreSQL, PDF, DOCX, TXT and Markdown documents, email through SMTP, and webhooks. Other integrations are discussed per need.
